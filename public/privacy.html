Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 2026. This Privacy Policy governs your use of Anizon Media Network services and explains how information is collected, protected, and utilized.

1. Introduction & Overview

At Anizon Media Network ("Anizon", "DarkPAKI", "we", "us", or "our"), user privacy is fundamental to our service design. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal and anonymous data gathered when you access our cinema showcases, media streaming tools, and community portal.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We partner with third-party vendors, including Google, to serve advertisements on our web properties:

  • Google and Third-Party Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide authentic, bot-free services:

  • Account Information: When registering, you provide your name, Gmail address, and phone number for 2-step verification.
  • Device Fingerprinting & Hardware Binding: To prevent multi-accounting fraud and protect community coin distribution, anonymous cryptographic hashes of browser capabilities are collected.
  • Log Data: Server logs record browser type, referring URLs, timestamps, and IP addresses for security diagnostics and rate limiting.

4. Data Security & Storage

All passwords and authentication tokens are encrypted using state-of-the-art bcrypt hashing and cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT). We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing brokers.

5. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and California enjoy specific rights regarding their personal data, including the right to access, rectify, delete, or export personal records. You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting us at our compliance desk.
